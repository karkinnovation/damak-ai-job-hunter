# Awasar v5

- Job seekers now see their top 2 matching vacancies immediately on the dashboard after login.
- Top 2 scores are deterministic and render without waiting for Gemini.
- Each dashboard match shows score, salary, employment type, top match reasons and a direct job link.
- The old prominent “Find My Best Matches” dashboard button is replaced by a secondary “View all matches” link.
- Incomplete profiles get a clear CTA: “Build your job profile and find the best match.” / “Create profile now”.
- Homepage matching CTA uses the same clearer wording.
