# 5-minute hackathon demo checklist

1. Open landing page: explain “right jobs, not more jobs.”
2. Login as the prepared job seeker.
3. Open profile briefly: show skills, salary, availability and Damak ward/travel preference.
4. Click **Hunt Jobs For Me**.
5. Point out that scores differ and explanations show both matches and mismatches.
6. Open the top vacancy and apply.
7. Logout; login as employer.
8. Open the vacancy and **Applicants**.
9. Show the same compatibility engine ranking candidates in reverse.
10. Explicitly state: “AI recommends; the employer makes the final decision.”
11. Shortlist the top candidate manually.

## Good prepared seeker

- Damak-5
- +2 Management
- Excel, Computer Basics, Accounting, Customer Service
- 8 months experience
- NPR 18,000–25,000
- Full-time
- 09:00–18:00
- 5 km travel preference
- Preferred category: Accounting / Finance

## Good prepared vacancies

Create deliberately different fits so the ranking looks real:

- Account Assistant: very strong match
- Computer Operator: strong/good match
- Office Assistant: medium-good match
- Salesperson: medium match
- Graphic Designer: low match

Avoid making every vacancy score above 85%.
